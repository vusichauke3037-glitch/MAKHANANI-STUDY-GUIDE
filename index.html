📗 GRADE 10 — FOUNDATION

Learn the basics. If you master Grade 10, Grade 11 & 12 are easy.

💰 1. FINANCE — Grade 10

Profit = Income - Expenses  |  Net = Gross - Deductions  |  VAT = 15% × Price excl
Example 1 — Budget: Income R5 000, Expenses R4 200. Profit = 5000 - 4200 = R800 Surplus
Example 2 — VAT: Price R400 excl. VAT = 15% × 400 = R60. Incl = R460
Example 3 — Payslip: Gross R8 000, Tax R800, UIF R80. Net = 8000 - 800 - 80 = R7 120
Exam Tip: Always show Formula + Substitution + Answer + R. Budget question: If Expenses > Income = Deficit.

📏 2. MEASUREMENT — Grade 10

Area Rectangle = L × B  |  Perimeter = 2(L+B)  |  1m = 100cm  |  1L = 1000ml
Example: Room 4m × 3m. Area = 12m². Perimeter = 2(4+3) = 14m
Conversion: 2.5 litres = 2500 ml  |  3km = 3000m

🗺️ 3. MAPS — Grade 10

Real Distance = Map Distance × Scale
Example: Map 4cm, Scale 1:25 000. Real = 4 × 25 000 = 100 000cm = 1km
Direction: North = Up, South = Down, East = Right, West = Left.

📊 4. DATA — Grade 10

Mean = Sum ÷ n  |  Range = Max - Min  |  Median = Middle (Sort First!)
Example: Data 5, 8, 10, 12. Sum=35. Mean = 35÷4 = 8.75. Range = 12-5 = 7. Mode = No mode (all once).

🎲 5. PROBABILITY — Grade 10

P(Event) = Favourable ÷ Total
Example: Bag 3 red, 5 blue. Total 8. P(red) = 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%

📘 GRADE 11 — INTERMEDIATE

New: Compound Interest, Hire Purchase, Exchange, Cylinder Volume, Tree Diagrams.

💰 1. FINANCE — Grade 11

I = P × r × n (Simple)  |  A = P(1+r)ⁿ (Compound)  |  Total HP = Deposit + (Monthly × Months)
Simple: R5 000 at 10% for 3 yrs. I = 5000×0.10×3 = R1 500. Amount = R6 500
Compound: R5 000 at 10% for 2 yrs. A = 5000(1.10)² = R6 050
Hire Purchase: Cash R4 000. HP: Deposit R800 + 12 × R350 = R5 000. Interest = R1 000
Exchange: Change R3 400 to $. Rate $1 = R17. $ = 3400 ÷ 17 = $200
Inflation: Bread R20, Inflation 6% for 2 yrs. New = 20×(1.06)² = R22.47
Exam Tip: Compound = Bigger than Simple. Always convert % to decimal: 10% = 0.10. r must match period.

📏 2. MEASUREMENT — Grade 11

Volume Box = L × B × H  |  Volume Cylinder = πr²h  |  Area Circle = πr²  |  π = 3.14
Example: Cylinder r=7cm, h=10cm. Volume = 3.14 × 49 × 10 = 1 539.8 cm³

🗺️ 3. MAPS — Grade 11

Distance = Speed × Time  |  Time = Distance ÷ Speed
Example: Travel 120km/h for 2.5h. Distance = 120 × 2.5 = 300km

📊 4. DATA — Grade 11

IQR = Q3 - Q1
Example: Q1=20, Q3=60. IQR = 40. Learn Box-and-Whisker + Histogram.

🎲 5. PROBABILITY — Grade 11

P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)  |  P(Not A) = 1 - P(A)
Example: Coin + Die. P(Head and 6) = 1/2 × 1/6 = 1/12

🔥 GRADE 12 — MATRIC — FINAL EXAM

Grade 12 = Grade 10 + 11 combined, harder. Master these 6 and you pass.

💰 1. FINANCE — Grade 12 (MOST MARKS)

Break-Even = Fixed ÷ (Sell - Variable)  |  A = P(1+r)ⁿ  |  Foreign = Rand ÷ Rate
Break-Even: Fixed R3 000, Variable R20, Sell R50. Break-Even = 3000 ÷ (50-20) = 3000 ÷ 30 = 100 units. Must sell 100 to not lose money.
Loan: Loan R20 000 at 12% p.a. compounded monthly for 2 yrs (24 months). r = 0.01. A = 20000(1.01)²⁴ = R25 394.66
Exchange + Commission: R5 000, Rate R18, Commission 2% = R100. Foreign = (5000-100) ÷ 18 = $272.22
Tariffs: Read tariff table. First 50kWh at R2, next at R3, etc. Add steps.
Exam Tip: Payslip question: Gross - Tax - UIF (1%) - Pension = Net. Tariff: Always calculate step-by-step, don't just multiply total.

📏 2. MEASUREMENT — Grade 12

1m³ = 1000 litres  |  Surface Area Box = 2(LB + LH + BH)
Example: Tank 2m³ = 2000 litres. Fuel: 400km at 8L/100km = 32L. Cost = 32 × R23 = R736

🗺️ 3. MAPS — Grade 12

Example — Floor Plan: Scale 1:100. Room 5cm on plan = 500cm = 5m real. Area = 5m × 4m = 20m². Tiles needed = Area ÷ Tile Area.

📊 4. DATA — Grade 12

Example: Graph shows sales increase Jan-Mar. Explain: "Sales increased because of back-to-school." Always explain WHAT graph shows, not just numbers. Watch for Outlier (one value far from others).

🎲 5. PROBABILITY — Grade 12

P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)
Example: Tree diagram with 2 stages: Draw branches, multiply along branches, add across branches.

🎓 GRADE 12 EXAM STRATEGY

Paper 1 (2hrs): Start with Finance, then Data. Paper 2 (2hrs): Start with Maps, then Probability.
Time: 1 mark = 1 minute. 100 marks = 100 min + 20 min check.
Never leave blank. Write formula = 1 mark.

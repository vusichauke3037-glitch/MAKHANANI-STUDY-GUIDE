📐 MATHS LIT — Grade 10-12 — CAPS

6 Topics. Finance = 40% of exam. Master Finance = Pass.

📗 MATHS LIT GRADE 10

Profit = Income - Expenses | Net = Gross - Deductions | VAT = 15% x Price excl
Finance: Income R5 000, Expenses R4 200. Profit = R800
VAT: R400 excl. VAT = R60. Incl = R460
Payslip: Gross R8 000, Tax R800, UIF R80. Net = R7 120
Area Rectangle = L x B | Perimeter = 2(L+B) | 1m = 100cm | 1L = 1000ml
Measurement: Room 4m x 3m. Area = 12m². Perimeter = 14m
Maps: Map 4cm, Scale 1:25 000. Real = 1km
Data: 5,8,10,12. Mean = 8.75. Range = 7
Probability: Bag 3 red, 5 blue. P(red) = 3/8 = 37.5%
Exam Tip: Always show Formula + Substitution + Answer + R. Budget: Expenses > Income = Deficit.

📘 MATHS LIT GRADE 11

I = P x r x n (Simple) | A = P(1+r)ⁿ (Compound) | Total HP = Deposit + (Monthly x Months)
Simple: R5 000 at 10% for 3 yrs. I = R1 500. Amount = R6 500
Compound: R5 000 at 10% for 2 yrs. A = R6 050
Hire Purchase: Deposit R800 + 12 x R350 = R5 000. Interest = R1 000
Exchange: R3 400 to $. Rate $1 = R17. $ = $200
Inflation: Bread R20, 6% for 2 yrs. New = R22.47
Cylinder: r=7, h=10. Volume = 3.14 x 49 x 10 = 1 539.8 cm³
Maps: 120km/h for 2.5h. Distance = 300km
Probability: Coin + Die. P(Head and 6) = 1/2 x 1/6 = 1/12
Exam Tip: Compound > Simple. Convert % to decimal: 10% = 0.10.

🔥 MATHS LIT GRADE 12 — MATRIC

Break-Even = Fixed ÷ (Sell - Variable) | A = P(1+r)ⁿ | Foreign = Rand ÷ Rate
Break-Even: Fixed R3 000, Variable R20, Sell R50. Break-Even = 3000 ÷ 30 = 100 units
Loan: R20 000 at 12% p.a. monthly for 2 yrs. A = 20000(1.01)²⁴ = R25 394.66
Exchange + Commission: R5 000, Rate R18, Comm 2% = R100. Foreign = (5000-100) ÷ 18 = $272.22
Tariffs: Step-by-step. First 50kWh at R2, next at R3, etc. Don't just multiply total.
Measurement: Tank 2m³ = 2000 litres. Fuel: 400km at 8L/100km = 32L. Cost = 32 x R23 = R736
Maps: Floor Plan Scale 1:100. 5cm = 5m real. Area = 20m²
Probability: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B). Tree: Multiply along, Add across.
Matric Strategy: Paper 1: Finance first, then Data. Paper 2: Maps first, then Probability. 1 mark = 1 minute. Never blank.

∑ MATHEMATICS — Grade 8-12 — CAPS

Algebra • Functions • Geometry • Trigonometry • Statistics • Finance

📘 GRADE 8-9 — FOUNDATION

BODMAS: Brackets → Of → Division / Multiplication → Addition / Subtraction
Area Triangle = ½ x base x height | Perimeter = Sum of sides | Volume Cube = s³
Integers: -5 + 8 = 3 | -3 x -4 = 12 | 15 - (-5) = 20
Fractions: 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6
Algebra: 3x + 5x = 8x | 2(x+3) = 2x + 6
Solve: x + 7 = 12. x = 5
Ratio: 2:3 of 50. Share = 50 ÷ 5 = 10. 2x10 = 20, 3x10 = 30
Geometry: Angles on straight line = 180°. Angles around point = 360°. Triangle = 180°
Tip: Always check by substituting answer back. If x=5, then 5+7=12 ✅

📗 GRADE 10 — MATHEMATICS

Factorise: ax + ay = a(x+y) | x² - y² = (x+y)(x-y) | (x+a)(x+b) = x² + (a+b)x + ab
Distance = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²] | Midpoint = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2) | Gradient m = (y₂-y₁)/(x₂-x₁)
Factorise: x² - 9 = (x+3)(x-3) | x² + 5x + 6 = (x+2)(x+3)
Solve: 2x - 5 = 11. 2x = 16. x = 8
Functions: f(x) = 2x + 3. f(4) = 11
Trig: sin θ = Opp/Hyp | cos θ = Adj/Hyp | tan θ = Opp/Adj
Pythagoras: a² + b² = c². 3-4-5 triangle: 3² + 4² = 5²

📘 GRADE 11 — MATHEMATICS

Quadratic Formula: x = [-b ± √(b²-4ac)] / 2a
Exponential: aᵐ x aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ | a⁰ = 1 | a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ
Trig: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 | tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
Quadratic: x² - 5x + 6 = 0. (x-2)(x-3)=0. x = 2 or 3
Exponents: 2³ x 2² = 2⁵ = 32 | (3²)³ = 3⁶
Functions: Parabola y = x². Vertex (0,0). Hyperbola y = 1/x. Exponential y = 2ˣ.
Finance: A = P(1+i)ⁿ. R10 000 at 10% for 2 yrs = R12 100

🔥 GRADE 12 — MATHEMATICS — MATRIC

Calculus: f'(x) = n xⁿ⁻¹ | Max/Min: f'(x)=0
Trig: sin(A+B) = sinA cosB + cosA sinB
Sequences: Arithmetic Tₙ = a + (n-1)d | Geometric Tₙ = a rⁿ⁻¹
Probability: P(A or B) = P(A)+P(B)-P(A and B)
Calculus: f(x) = x² + 3x. f'(x) = 2x + 3. At x=2, gradient = 7
Sequences: 2,5,8,11... d=3. Tₙ = 2 + (n-1)3. T₁₀ = 29
Trig Equation: sin x = 0.5. x = 30° or 150°
Finance: Annuity: Future Value = Pmt x [(1+i)ⁿ -1]/i
Matric Tip: Paper 1 = Algebra + Calculus + Finance. Paper 2 = Trig + Geometry + Stats. Show all steps.

⚛️ PHYSICAL SCIENCES — Grade 10-12 — CAPS

Physics + Chemistry. Formula + Units + Direction. g = 9.8 m/s²

📗 GRADE 10 — PHYSICAL SCIENCES

PHYSICS: v = d / t | a = (v - u) / t | F = ma | W = mg (g=9.8) | Density = m / V
CHEMISTRY: n = m / M | C = n / V | pH < 7 = Acid | pH > 7 = Base
Speed: 100m in 10s. v = 10 m/s
Force: m=5kg, a=2 m/s². F = 5 x 2 = 10 N
Weight: m=10kg. W = 10 x 9.8 = 98 N
Moles: Mass 20g, M=10 g/mol. n = 20 ÷ 10 = 2 mol
Acids: Vinegar = Acid. Soap = Base. Water = Neutral (pH 7)

📘 GRADE 11 — PHYSICAL SCIENCES

PHYSICS: V = IR (Ohm's Law) | P = VI | E = mcΔT | v = f λ
CHEMISTRY: Balancing: Reactants = Products | Molar Volume = 22.4 L/mol at STP
Ohm's Law: V=12V, R=4Ω. I = V/R = 3 A
Power: V=12V, I=3A. P = 36 W
Waves: f=10Hz, λ=2m. v = 10 x 2 = 20 m/s
Balancing: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
Energy: m=2kg, c=4200, ΔT=10. E = 2 x 4200 x 10 = 84 000 J

🔥 GRADE 12 — PHYSICAL SCIENCES — MATRIC

PHYSICS: F = ma | W = Fd | Eₖ = ½mv² | Eₚ = mgh | Momentum p = mv
CHEMISTRY: Rate = Change / Time | Kₐ, K_b | E°cell = E°cathode - E°anode
Kinetic Energy: m=2kg, v=10 m/s. Eₖ = ½ x 2 x 100 = 100 J
Momentum: m=5kg, v=4 m/s. p = 20 kg·m/s
Work: F=50N, d=10m. W = 500 J
Galvanic Cell: Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ (Anode, Oxidation). Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu (Cathode, Reduction)
Matric Tip: Paper 1 = Physics. Paper 2 = Chemistry. Always include units + direction (e.g. 10 m/s East).

🌱 NATURAL SCIENCES — Grade 8-9 — CAPS

Life Sciences + Earth + Matter & Materials + Energy. Key Words = Marks.

📗 GRADE 8 — NATURAL SCIENCES

Photosynthesis: CO₂ + H₂O + Sunlight → Glucose + O₂
Respiration: Glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + Energy
Food Web: Producer → Consumer → Decomposer
Photosynthesis: Where? Chloroplast (Leaf). Needs: Sunlight, Water, CO₂.
Cells: Animal Cell = Nucleus, Cytoplasm, Membrane. Plant Cell = + Cell Wall + Chloroplast + Vacuole.
Ecosystem: Producer = Grass. Primary Consumer = Rabbit. Secondary = Fox.
Matter: Solid → Liquid = Melting. Liquid → Gas = Evaporation / Boiling. Gas → Liquid = Condensation.
Energy: Kinetic = Moving. Potential = Stored. Heat flows Hot → Cold.

📘 GRADE 9 — NATURAL SCIENCES

Force: F = ma | Pressure = Force / Area | Speed = Distance / Time
Electricity: V = IR | Series: Current same | Parallel: Voltage same
Chemical: Acid + Base → Salt + Water
Force: m=10kg, a=2 m/s². F = 20 N
Pressure: F=100N, Area=2m². P = 50 Pa
Acid + Base: HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O (Salt + Water)
Earth: Rock Cycle: Igneous → Sedimentary → Metamorphic. Plate Tectonics = Moving plates cause Earthquakes + Volcanoes.
Human Body: Heart = Pumps blood. Lungs = Take in O₂, give out CO₂. Brain = Controls body.
Tip: In Natural Sciences, DEFINITIONS = Marks. Learn: Photosynthesis, Evaporation, Condensation, Ecosystem, Producer, Consumer.
